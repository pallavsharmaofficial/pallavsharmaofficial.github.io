A relationship counsellor that runs entirely on the phone.

Source

Why it is built this way

What people would type into a relationship counsellor is the last thing that should sit on somebody’s server. So none of it does: the whole thing runs on the handset, with no account and nothing uploaded.

What it does

Guided conversations, reflection prompts and exercises drawn from counselling practice, in English and Hindi, free and offline.

  • Runs entirely on the device
  • No account, nothing uploaded
  • Hindi and English
  • Free
