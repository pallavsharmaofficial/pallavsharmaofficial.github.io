A job hunt run like a campaign, for the weeks of a notice period.

Source

The idea

A notice period is a fixed, countable window, and most people spend it anxious rather than organised. This turns it into a plan with a date on it.

What it does

A daily plan that adapts to how many days are left, mock interviews that are scored rather than just recorded, and a resume review. Offline by default, English and Hindi.

  • Daily plan against your last date
  • Scored mock interviews
  • Resume review
  • Offline by default
