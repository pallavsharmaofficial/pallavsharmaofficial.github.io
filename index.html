KundliSaar Flutter · Dart

A Vedic astrology app with its own ephemeris. Rather than licence Swiss Ephemeris — which would have forced the whole project open under AGPL — I fitted a Poisson series to JPL's DE440s and emitted it as Dart tables. Planetary longitudes land within about an arcsecond from 1900 to 2070, computed on the handset with the radio off.

On top of that: sixteen divisional charts, Vimshottari to three levels with dated periods, a full panchang checked against published almanacs to the minute, Tajika varshphal, ashtakavarga, shadbala, and a predictive layer that names the chart factor behind every sentence it writes.

  • 450+ tests, verified against skyfield and DE440s
  • 7,084 Indian towns, searchable without diacritics
  • Hindi and English throughout; nothing leaves the device
